The Metals

Metals that Nourish & Adorn

Every P•TAL metal has a purpose — copper for water, brass for cooking, kansa for dining. The materials are as important as the craft.

Copper

Pure copper waterware naturally balances pH and supports digestion. It is ideal for daily hydration and rituals.

  • Purifies water naturally
  • Supports immunity
  • Polishes with age

Brass

Brass brings warmth to cooking and serving. It is durable, beautifully luminous and a traditional kitchen essential.

  • Non-stick with care
  • Even heat distribution
  • Elegant, rich glow

Kansa

Kansa is Ayurveda's most auspicious dining metal. It is prized for calming digestion and giving food a subtle mineral embrace.

  • Balances digestion
  • Softens acidic meals
  • Refined golden finish

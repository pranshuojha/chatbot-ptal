Elevate your dining experience with this 6-piece hand-hammered brass dinner set. Crafted in Jandiala Guru by master Thathera artisans, this set doesn't just serve food—it serves a 400-year-old living history. Perfect for hosting or gifting.

Each piece is hand-hammered by the Thathera community of Punjab. This traditional craft is the only craft form from India to be inscribed on the UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage List.
Wash with a mild detergent and a soft sponge. To maintain the golden shine, rub with a mixture of tamarind and salt or use Pitambari powder periodically. Wipe dry immediately after washing.

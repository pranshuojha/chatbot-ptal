Ayurvedic Science

Three Metals,
Three Wisdoms

P•TAL works with three of Ayurveda's most revered metals — each with distinct healing properties, distinct heritage and distinct beauty.

Copper

Tamba · For Drinking & Water

Ayurveda has prescribed storing water in copper vessels overnight for millennia. Modern science confirms: copper ions naturally destroy bacteria and enrich water with essential trace minerals.

Purifies drinking water naturally
Anti-microbial & anti-inflammatory
Rich in trace minerals
Warm hue that deepens beautifully with age

Brass

Pital · For Cooking & Eating

Brass (70% Copper + 30% Zinc) is the traditional metal of Indian kitchens. Naturally non-stick and completely free of Teflon — it distributes heat evenly and imparts subtle mineral richness to every dish.

Naturally non-stick — zero Teflon
Enriches food with Zinc & Copper
Even, gentle heat distribution
Lasts generations with simple care

Kansa

Bronze · For Eating & Wellbeing

Kansa (Bell Metal) is revered in Ayurveda as the most auspicious metal for eating. Said to neutralise acidic foods, balance all three doshas and aid in calm, peaceful digestion after every meal.

Alkalises acidic foods naturally
Balances Vata, Pitta & Kapha
Promotes calm digestion
Subtle golden tone that ages with grace
